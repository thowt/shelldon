Shelldon SSH is a free, open-source SSH client for Android. It is in early development.

Save your servers once and connect with one tap, or type user@host to connect on the spot. Sessions stay alive while you switch apps. It supports public-key authentication (including hardware security keys), port forwarding, jump hosts, terminal color schemes and fonts, and a per-host crypto policy, so older devices such as legacy switches and routers stay reachable only when you choose it for that host.

No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no account.

Shelldon SSH is based on the ConnectBot project and is licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
